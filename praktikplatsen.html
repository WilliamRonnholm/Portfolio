New internal system to manage internships for schools in Gothenburg

Offering, applying and administering prao should be easy! That's why the Gothenburg Region (GR) has developed Praktikplatsen.se, which is used for coordinating internships within various school forms, including prao. Here, employers can register their school places and get an overview of bookings, and students can search for school places themselves. Education providers can coordinate their prao periods, everything from applications to placements and contacts in one place.

I was brought in together with a colleague to create the new Praktikplatsen.se. The internal system needed improvements in terms of structure and navigation along with it´s updated visual identity.
