General

I am William Rönnholm, a 32 year old UX/UI designer. I have a passion for creating user-centered designs that are both beautiful and functional. My focus is on developing intuitive and engaging user experiences that meet business objectives. In my free time, I enjoy travelling, working out, football and learning new design tools and techniques.

Experience

I have several years of experience in UX/UI design, working with both small startups and large corporations. My previous projects have included designing web and internal systems. I am skilled in user research, wireframing, prototyping and visual design, and experienced in collaborating with cross-functional teams, including developers, product managers and business stakeholders.
